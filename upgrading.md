# Upgrading guide!
# FOR USERS WHO WANT TO UPGRADE FROM ANY IOS VERSION BEFORE BETA 4!
# warning and heads up: If you have a replaced screen and or battery, please do upgrading carefully, because your phone might not work afterwards. this only occurs with aftermarket parts, not with Apple Original parts. We do not own any responsibility for your device's state, use, and follow this guide at your own risk.

### Step 1: Get your IPSW:

- go to https://ipsw.dev
- select your device type, for example: iPhone
- select your device model: for example iPhone 17
- select iOS 27 Beta 4 (last version which is mond compatible with, and the most stable one.)

### Step 2: Upgrade without data loss. Mac edition: (scroll further if you have MS Windows.)

- once your IPSW downloaded head over to Finder
- Plug your iDevice in
- Once your device pops up, open it, click Option (alt) on your Keyboard, while holding Option, click Check for an Update.
- Select the IPSW in the file selector.
- Once Finder says that update is done, please keep your device plugged in until your device boots into iOS.
- Wait, and iOS 27 Beta 4 will welcome you.

### Step 2: Windows edition.

- USE ITUNES!! Download iTunes from Apple
- Plug your iPhone in to your Windows PC.
- Open iTunes, navigate to your device.
- Click Shift, while holding down Shift, click check for updates.
- Select the IPSW file.
- wait until your iPhone fully boots up. dont unplug your phone in this process, you will corrupt it.
